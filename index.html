20/10/2026:Biryani

Biryani is a rich, aromatic mixed rice dish that originated in the Indian subcontinent from Persian roots. It is made by layering fluffy, long-grain basmati rice with a tender, spiced meat—such as chicken, mutton, or beef—or vegetables, infused with fragrant spices like cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, and saffron. These components are slow-cooked together using the dum method so that the rich juices and flavors meld deeply into every grain. Often served alongside cooling raita or gravy, biryani is a centerpiece of grand celebrations, weddings, and family gatherings around the world.

23/10/2026:Pizza

Pizza is a popular Italian dish made with a flat, baked crust topped with tomato sauce, melted cheese, and various meats or vegetables. Originating in Naples, Italy, it has grown into a global favorite known for its warm, gooey cheese and endless topping options. Whether baked in a high-heat wood-fired oven or ordered for a casual night in, its simple combination of savory flavors makes it a beloved meal for all ages.
